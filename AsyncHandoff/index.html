About these selected visualizations

Chinese requests appear alongside the English translations actually used by the generators. Each recording follows one continuously running player at its original timing, without within-action cuts or speedup. All requested target frames are retained in the raw motion record.

The rendered body uses an anatomically constrained SMPL-H reconstruction and display-only temporal smoothing and contact-aware floor retargeting. The videos therefore illustrate execution rather than unprocessed kinematic quality. Per-case controller settings, fitting errors, seeds and raw motion are available above. These independent selected cases are separate from the frozen benchmark results below and do not constitute a matched comparison of the three providers.

01 — Motivation

A complete action
should not stop the stream.

A non-streaming model can prepare an expressive, complete action. An interactive character still needs something to play while that action is being generated—and a way to keep moving afterward.

How do we reuse the full action without giving up continuous execution?

Streaming T2M

Generates successive motion segments and can respond during ongoing playback. The action is synthesized through the streaming model.

Non-streaming T2M

Prepares a complete clip before playback. Entry from the current motion and continuation afterward remain integration questions.

AsyncHandoff

Responds with streaming motion, connects to the complete external action, preserves it, then resumes streaming from its final history.

02 — Method

Prepare ahead. Hand off at the anchor.

The connector refines an analytic entry bridge using the reserved motion history and target context. The complete target bypasses the learned output. During target playback, its final history conditions prefetched streaming continuation.

01

Respond

The frozen streaming generator supplies the immediate response while the target is prepared.

02

Connect

A lightweight network adds bounded corrections to an analytic bridge at a reserved future boundary.

03

Preserve

The target follows through an independent path, retaining every native frame in order.

04

Continue

The target’s last 13 frames condition the frozen streaming model; no exit connector is used in the main method.
